Click a row to copy and paste!


Intended Convention: 
- use ONLY tab to separate each term.   (please replace all instances of "\t" w/ another delimeter if you want to use tabs as characters)
    Ex: [character(s) terms]\t\t[possible name(s)]\t\t[tag(s)]
- Character: a symbol to copy and paste. customized strings with multiple characters are allowed
    Ex: e^(i𝜋)	e^i𝜋	e^i𝜋 + 1 = 0      euler's identity   i haven't actually seen this       math
- Name: a search term
    Ex: searching "qu" may show characters named with "equal" and "quantifier" but not "q"
    STRICT. Cannot handle spaces, currently does not allow searching multiple names. 
    Note that there's a line that trims spaces before & after names.
- Tags: filter intended to group characters together
    Ex: "discrete mathematics" symbols include ∧ ∨ ∅ ∀ etc...

- named starting with "~" so unimportant characters are at the bottom
- repeated characters (multiple lines) are allowed, but may create duplicate lines
    this is useful for tagging multiple alphabets together (to avoid copy pasting tags 26 x "how many alphabets are there?" times)
    or vice versa (ex: naming all "A" characters)
    
No thought was put into optimizing.
Please modify data at your own requirements :)
